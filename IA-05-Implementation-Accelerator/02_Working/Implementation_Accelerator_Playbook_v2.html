Readiness is a human decision

The accelerator organizes evidence and highlights gaps. It does not independently approve a learner for customer work.

1. LearnRequired modules and source-linked checks completed.
2. PracticeScenario decisions demonstrate reasoning, not memorization.
3. ValidateManager or SME observes judgment in live or shadowed work.
4. ApproveLeader determines readiness and assigns mentor support.

Update workflow

A simple path from change request to published guidance.

1
ProposeSubmit the changed step, reason, affected role/product/stage, and source.
2
Owner reviewProfessional Services owner confirms the process change.
3
SME validateProduct or process expert checks nuance, timing, and exceptions.
4
PublishVersion, effective date, and affected modules are updated together.
5
NotifyLearners and leaders see what changed and whether retraining is needed.
