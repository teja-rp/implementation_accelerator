### Verified in the current HTML

- Six navigation views and six module cards are present.
- Each module exposes Learn, Do, Practice, and Ask Accelerator sections.
- The implementation-planning checklist blocks module completion until every required item is selected.
- Ask Accelerator produces a scripted module-specific response and displays source links.
- The knowledge-change control displays the intended source/owner/SME/version workflow.
- The experience labels learner, customer, project, and leadership data as fictional or sanitized.

### What remains unproven or incomplete

- The Ask Accelerator interaction is a scripted prototype, not a live retrieval-augmented AI service.
- Progress is device-local only; there is no shared learner record, authentication, or multi-user leadership data.
- Leadership metrics are fictional and must not be presented as measured outcomes.
- Product and process content has not yet received final owner/SME approval.
- The SME session recording or notes have not yet been incorporated into the source set.
- Source-effective dates and accountable owners are incomplete in some materials, including the EC SOP.
- A full accessibility, security, cross-browser, and production-hosting review has not been completed.
- A narrow-screen test identified horizontal overflow that should be corrected before calling the prototype mobile-ready.
- The update/version workflow is represented visually but is not connected to a live content-management process.

## Decisions and assumptions

- **Decision:** Keep the smallest useful prototype focused on Engagement Coordinator onboarding for add-on customer work.  
  **Why:** This matches the brief's scope boundary while leaving an extensible model for Engagement Managers and new-logo implementations.

- **Decision:** Make the learning journey the primary experience and place project application in a separate **Apply to work** view.  
  **Why:** The original portfolio-first layout made the artifact feel like an operational dashboard rather than a full playbook.

- **Decision:** Use one repeatable module pattern: **Learn -> Do -> Practice -> Ask Accelerator**.  
  **Why:** This makes the playbook easier to understand, scale, maintain, and demonstrate.

- **Decision:** Treat readiness as a manager/SME judgment, not an automated result of module completion.  
  **Why:** The brief requires human SME review and preservation of Professional Services judgment.

- **Decision:** Keep all customer, learner, progress, and leadership examples sanitized or fictional.  
  **Why:** The prototype should demonstrate value without exposing customer information or implying actual measured results.

- **Assumption needing confirmation:** The six-module lifecycle and two-week mapping are the correct minimum sequence for the hackathon demo.
- **Assumption needing confirmation:** The OneSite, Payments, Loft Living, and Loft Leasing dependency example is current and sufficiently representative for the demo.
- **Assumption needing confirmation:** The intended audience wants the prototype to show both new-hire onboarding and in-work guidance in one experience.
- **Assumption needing confirmation:** A static interactive HTML prototype is an acceptable final artifact or demo component.
