SUMMARY
The Engagement Coordinator (EC) is responsible for overseeing the successful deployment of the RealPage platform. The EC partners with each assigned customer to understand their desired implementation timeline then creates a deployment schedule to meet that timeline. The EC works with each Implementation resource assigned to the project to ensure the RealPage products are scheduled to deploy concurrently, and as efficiently as possible.

The EC is responsible for providing weekly updates to the customer via email on the status of the project. This includes the agreed upon deployment schedule, as well as the status of the deliverables needed to deploy each product. The EC will streamline the deliverable collection process and provide a comprehensive update on what has been received and what is still outstanding.

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES
Oversee deployment/implementation of RealPage products for assigned clients
Manage project management plans for Add-on and SMB Customers
Partner with RP implementation resources to provide a streamlined onboarding experience
Create consolidated deployment schedule
Provide deliverables tracking across the RealPage solutions
Provide weekly project updates to customers via email

REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE/SKILLS/ABILITIES
BA/BS degree or equivalent experience within industry
Proficiency with MS Office Tools
Strong project management skills required; ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously
One year of proven effectiveness leading and facilitating meetings
Ability to prioritize, multitask and perform effectively under pressure
Strong attention to detail
Ability to adapt to change and work well in a dynamic environment

PREFERRED KNOWLEDGE/SKILLS/ABILITIES
Understanding of and experience with multifamily industry operations and marketing
Knowledge of RealPage products and platform features, integrations and best practices

REQUIRED COMPETENCIES

Communication: Speak, listen and write in a clear, thorough and timely manner using appropriate and effective communication tools and techniques
Collaborating: Work cooperatively and effectively with others to set goals, resolve problems, and make decisions that enhance organizational effectiveness
Planning and Organizing: Set priorities, develop an action plan, monitor progress towards goals and track details/data/information/activities as directed
Adaptability: Maintain effectiveness and adjust accordingly when major change occurs
