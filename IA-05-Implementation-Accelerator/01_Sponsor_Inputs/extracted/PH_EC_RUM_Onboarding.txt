=== Slide 1 ===
RUM
FOR ENGAGEMENT MANAGERS AND
ENGAGEMENT COORDINATORS
[Speaker notes]
It’s important that our solutions paint a picture in your mind that allows you to develop a framework that work as one with the clients.
RealPage tie all our services together beautifully begins with OneSite. This is the software where they manage their resident records with move-ins, move-outs, annual recertifications, evictions, etc.
After OneSite, We have resident portals. These are commonly known as LOFT Living, LOFT Leasing and Online Renewals. Now, in these portals, residents need to make Payments. To make this possible, we are implementing RealPage Payments. Payments and these resident portals become a powerful tool for the clients to make it convenient for them to collect rent and utility payments.
One of the group of solutions that our clients are interested in are the Utility Management Services also known as RUM or RUSM.

=== Slide 2 ===
What is RealPage?
RP is a software company providing solutions for the real estate landscape.
What are RP services that work together as a healthy ecosystem of solutions that solve property management company problems?
[Speaker notes]
There are three main solutions in the RUM group:
Resident Billing – we simply divide the large client bill to bill the residents back.
For example, water will be billed back in two most convenient methods.
One is SUBS or submetered utility billing system. The client has to have a submetered system installed and ofcourse we have to be able to read these submeters. In the deliverables portion I will tell you what the client needs to provide.
Another is RUBS or ratio utility billing system – like 5 people is 50$ per month. Or 800sq ft of apartment is 40$ of water bill.
Utility Expense Management also called as bill pay when implemented allows us to pay all client utility bills enrolled in this solution. There are also clients that want us to just organize their bills for us and they will pay it to the utility providers directly.
VCR is where we make sure that all utility bills that need a name transfer like electricity commonly is under the name of the resident and doesn’t remain under the name of the property.
