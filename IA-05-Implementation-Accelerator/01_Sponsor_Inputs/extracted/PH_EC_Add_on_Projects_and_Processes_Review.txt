=== Slide 2 ===
New Logo vs Expansion vs Add-on
New Logo
A customer that is completely new to RealPage and has not previously licensed or utilized any RealPage products or services.
Expansion
An existing RealPage client (Property Management Company) that is adopting an additional solution for the first time, expanding beyond their currently licensed products.
Add-On
An existing RealPage client that already licenses a specific product and is extending its use by onboarding additional properties or sites to that same solution.
Add-On with Expansion
An existing RealPage client that is both expanding usage of an existing product to additional properties while also implementing a new solution for the first time.
