=== Slide 2 ===
01 / THE RULE
Separate setup from activation
A product may be prepared early even when its activation depends on another product.
1
Prepare
Configuration, data gathering, and readiness work can begin.
→
2
Activate
The product becomes usable once its required dependency is live.
→
3
Integrate
Connect the product to OneSite or other required products.
Coordinator mindset
Always ask: “Can we set it up now?” and “What must be live before we activate it?”

=== Slide 3 ===
02 / BEFORE ONESITE
Use the early window for parallel work
These products can move forward while the OneSite timeline is still being finalized.
Accounting Entity
Create anytime → integrate with OneSite
Payments
Setup can begin early → activate after OneSite is live
Knock
Can go live early → integrate with OneSite later
Website
Set up early → complete integrations later
Contact Center Answer Automation
Can go live prior to OneSite
eSupply
No product dependencies → go live anytime
Key watch-out: early setup does not automatically mean early activation.

04 / PAYMENTS PATH
Payments can be prepared early—but activation waits for OneSite
This is the most important sequencing distinction for resident-facing functionality.
1
Payments setup
May begin early
→
2
OneSite live
Required activation gate
→
3
Payments activated
Online payment capability enabled
→
4
Loft Leasing live
Requires activated Payments
Loft Living note
Loft Living requires OneSite and may go live before Payments. Residents cannot pay online until Payments is activated.

=== Slide 8 ===
07 / APPLY THE WORKFLOW
Coordinator checklist
Use these questions before confirming an implementation or go-live date.
✓
What can be set up in parallel before OneSite?
✓
Is OneSite live before activating Payments?
✓
Has the Screening scoring-model property been identified?
✓
Is Payments activated before Loft Leasing?
✓
Are Loft Loyalty and Loft Moving scheduled 3–5 days after Loft Living?
✓
Is the property date current before BI or Performance Analytics setup?
✓
Are all Website integration prerequisites live?
Outcome: a timeline that is executable, dependency-aware, and clear to the customer and internal teams.
