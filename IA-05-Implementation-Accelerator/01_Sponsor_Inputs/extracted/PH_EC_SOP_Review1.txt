=== Slide 12 ===
Engagement Intake & Readiness
Objectives/Goal
Ensure engagements are fully prepared before kickoff.
SLA: Within 1–2 business days of assignment.
Steps:
Review assigned orders
Send Sales Rep the EC Sales Prep Questionnaire (Sales Prep Questionnaire)
Confirm product(s), scope type, number of properties, and timelines.
Validate key client contacts and escalation paths.
Confirm consultant assignment as needed.
Determine assignment timelines.
Ensure required DataHub questionnaires are turned on.
Flag gaps, missing information, or readiness risks to leader, Sales Rep and /or CSM.
Prepare Project Tracker

=== Slide 13 ===
Scheduling & Coordination Project
Objective/Goal:Ensure timely and accurate scheduling of engagement activities.
Steps:
Send EC Intro email to the client. (Email Templates Folder)
SLA 1-3 business days from assignment,
Always copy assigned SalesRep
Schedule a kickoff call with the client to efficiently gather project information and minimize email back-and-forth.
Coordinate availability with client. (Email Templates Folder)
Send calendar invite (include your SR)
Send meeting agenda at least 1 business day before the meeting
Kickoff call workflow (will have a separate discussion)
Send Recap Email within 24 hours after conclusion

Client Communication
Objective/Goal:Maintain professional, clear, and consistent communication.
Guidelines:
Use RealPage-approved tone: professional, neutral, client-focused.
Respond or acknowledge client emails within 1 business day.
Keep communications factual and process-oriented.

EC Introductions/Kick off (Email Templates Folder)

DLSD communications
Consultant introductions
Scheduling coordination, confirmations and reminders
Data or document requests
Meeting agenda and recap
Client Email Communications (i.e. email replies)
Activations and Next-step summaries

=== Slide 18 ===
Risk & Issue Escalation
Objectives/Goal
Identify risks early to protect delivery timelines.
Common Risk Indicators:
Delayed client data or prerequisites
Repeated rescheduling
Low client responsiveness
Confusion around scope or deliverables
Delayed Consulting assignment
Delayed handoff and implementation
Process:
Document the issue clearly.
Notify team lead with impact and context.
Track resolution status until closed (EC Action Item Tracker).

=== Slide 19 ===
Billing & Administrative Support
Objectives/Goal
Support accurate engagement execution and billing readiness.
Support Activities:
Monitor backlog report – review statuses, forecast dates, etc. ensuring downstream communication to those needed when ..
ICD missing when product activation communicated, incorrect due to Cancellation, On Hold, Shelfware, etc
Connect with Sales for any missing orders
Ensure DLSD is clearly communicated with the client especially if there are delays in implementation

=== Slide 20 ===
End of Engagement
Objectives/Goal:
Ensure clean and complete engagement closure.
Steps:
Notify client of upcoming end of engagement, 2 weeks prior to estimated EoE date, including Sales Rep and CSM
Confirm all pending orders are completed or accounted for.
Send End of Engagement email. Include any pending products with assigned SC or POC. (End of Engagement Resources)
Log all challenges, issues encountered and accomplishments in our lessons learned file (EC Lessons Learned).

Out of Office Policy
Planned Leaves Checklist
Get approval from Team Lead
Once approved log request in the PH PTO Request Portal
Secure out of office buddy/ies
Send OOO calendar invite to Leader and all ECs
Notify client at least a week in advance about upcoming leave (1-2 days before PTO)
Notify SCs especially those with activations scheduled. Endorse expected activations or deliverables accordingly
Remind client about the upcoming PTO via email. Include pending items and OOO buddy details.
Setup a meeting with your buddy/ies to go over endorsements. Send a recap email detailing endorsements. Make sure TL is copied.
Setup your external and Internal OOO response. Template below.
Unplanned Leaves Checklist
Notify your team leader
If with access and capacity
Setup OOO notification if possible (use standard OOO template)
Put an OOO blocker on your calendar
Notify your clients and SCs
Reach out to colleagues who can be your out of office buddy/ies
