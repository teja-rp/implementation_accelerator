Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Role: Engagement Coordinator (EC)
Function: Professional Services – Engagement ManagementCompany: RealPage

1. Purpose
This SOP defines the responsibilities, workflows, tools, and quality standards for the Engagement Coordinator role at RealPage. The goal is to ensure consistent engagement readiness, accurate coordination, and high-quality client experience across all Professional Services engagements.
2. Scope
This SOP applies to all Engagement Coordinators supporting RealPage Professional Services engagements, including:
Add-ons with up to 3 functional groups
3. Role Overview
The Engagement Coordinator provides operational and coordination support to assigned projects. The role focuses on:
Engagement setup and readiness
Scheduling and coordination
Client-facing communication (predominantly email)
Documentation, tracking, and follow-through
Early risk identification and escalation
Throughout the deployment, the EC is responsible for coordinating implementations across all RealPage teams. They partner with the Solution Consultants to provide support and guidance for technical implementations, serving as the point of escalation to ensure deadlines are met, and client expectations are satisfied. The EC does not own scope or delivery strategy but plays a critical role in enabling on-time, high-quality execution.
4. Key Responsibilities
Support Add-on PMCs with day-to-day coordination activities
Maintain accurate engagement records in RealPage systems
Coordinate any project or product calls as needed
Communicate with clients regarding scheduling, next steps, and logistics
Coordinate with internal teams – Solution Consultants, configuration teams, Sales, CS Organization and/or Support to ensure timely implementations and activations
Track client dependencies and follow up as needed.
Monitor deliverables submission through DataHub and/or other deliverables collection tools
Escalate risks, delays, or readiness gaps promptly
5. Key Stakeholders
Clients (Property Management Companies and Stakeholders)
Implementation Consultants
Success Managers (CSM, SAM, DSAM, etc)
Account Reps (Sales)
Billing, Training, Support, Cancellations and Product Teams
6. Tools & Systems
Engagement Coordinators are expected to use the following RealPage-aligned tools:
Salesforce – engagement records, order details, key dates
Project tracking tools (e.g., Smartsheet, Excel)
Outlook – client and internal meeting coordination
SharePoint / OneDrive – project documentation and artifacts
Microsoft Teams – internal collaboration and updates
7. Training and Onboarding
7.1. Training Overview
All newly hired Engagement Coordinators (ECs) are required to complete a two-week structured onboarding training program designed to build foundational knowledge, tools proficiency, and execution readiness.
The training program follows the EC Onboarding Training Agenda and training sessions will be delivered through a combination of:
Live sessions facilitated by an assigned Manager, and/or
Recorded training modules available within onboarding materials
7.2. Shadowing and Hands-On Application
During Training
ECs will shadow ongoing EM-led Add-on Implementations
Purpose:
Reinforce training concepts through real-world application
Gain visibility into client interactions, deliverables, and timelines
Observe execution of project workflows and coordination practices
Post-Training
Designated Engagement Manager (EM) mentor/s will shadow the first 2-3 projects assigned to the EC. The assigned mentor is responsible for guiding the EC on:
Internal and external communications (client, stakeholders, Sales Consultants)
Execution of daily and weekly EC responsibilities
Management of implementation schedules and deliverables
Proper documentation and adherence to SOPs
7.3. Compliance Requirements
All ECs must complete:
Required system access setup (e.g., Salesforce, DataHub, OMS, Teams)
Mandatory RealPage compliance trainings (e.g., InfoSec, Anti-Harassment, Antitrust)
Onboarding checklist requirements prior to full project ownership

9. Standard Operating Procedures
9.1 Engagement Intake & Readiness
Objective: Ensure engagements are fully prepared before kickoff.
Steps:
Review assigned orders
Send Sales Rep the EC Sales Prep Questionnaire (Sales Prep Questionnaire)
Confirm product(s), scope type, number of properties, and timelines.
Validate key client contacts and escalation paths.
Confirm consultant assignment as needed.
Determine assignment timelines.
Ensure required DataHub questionnaires are turned on.
Flag gaps, missing information, or readiness risks to leader, Sales Rep and /or CSM.
Prepare Project Tracker
SLA: Within 1–2 business days of assignment.

9.2 Scheduling & Coordination Project
Objective: Ensure timely and accurate scheduling of engagement activities.
Steps:

Send EC Intro email to the client. (Email Templates Folder)
SLA 1-3 business days from assignment,
Always copy assigned SalesRep
It is strongly encouraged for ECs to schedule a kickoff call with the clients. This allows for a more efficient data gathering at the start of the project vs multiple emails
Coordinate availability with client. (Email Templates Folder)
Send calendar invite (include your SalesRep)
Send meeting agenda at least 1 business day before the meeting
Kickoff call workflow
Get permission to record the call
EC introduction highlighting role and that we are not product experts
Discuss client readiness and targets
Go through each product ordered discussing deliverables needed, due dates, product dependencies, implementation timelines and forecast dates
Set schedule for weekly implementation updates to be sent via email
Answer or take note of any questions
Close the meeting
Send recap email within 24 hours after the meeting
Confirm client timing needs (GO LIVE and readiness)
Affirm deliverable due dates to align with client requested GO LIVES, ensuring dependence alignment.
Confirm proposed timing with SC’s/Scheduling
Update trackers based on confirmation of dates
Send weekly Updates – Mainly through email but can set up weekly calls if needed (PMC Weekly Update Template)
Monitor deliverables submission and/or changes in timing. Notify internal teams and update project plan accordingly. Re-set client expectations on impact of missed timelines/targets.
Respond to emails and Inquiries, looping in appropriate Subject Matter Experts as necessary. SLA for response to client emails (acknowledge emails): 24 hours.
Schedule additional calls with the clients as needed
If a Solution Consultant or any other RealPage stakeholder is required to attend a call, please ensure you coordinate their availability before sharing time options with the client. As a best practice, place 2–3 tentative holds on their calendar and confirm alignment in advance prior to finalizing the meeting schedule with the client.
Always copy your assigned SalesRep
Send agenda before the meeting and recap after the meeting.
Identify client’s training needs and provide recommended available training options and resources with the help of the implementation teams/SCs.

9.3 Client Communication
Objective: Maintain professional, clear, and consistent communication.
Guidelines:
Use RealPage-approved tone: professional, neutral, client-focused.
Respond or acknowledge client emails within 1 business day.
Keep communications factual and process-oriented.
Typical EC Communications:
EC Introductions/Kick off (Email Templates Folder)
Weekly implementation updates (PMC Weekly Update Template)
DLSD communications
Consultant introductions
Scheduling coordination, confirmations and reminders
Data or document requests
Meeting agenda and recap
Client Email Communications (i.e. email replies)
Activations and Next-step summaries
End of Engagement
Meeting Invites:
Confirm SC availability before providing schedule options to the clients.
Send calendar invites with:
Subject Line – include purpose of call (ZRS – RealPage | Payments Kick Off)
Clear agenda – can be general if invite sent well in advance, but agenda to be refined/updated 24hrs prior to call.
Dial-in / Teams links
All required attendees
Escalation Rule: Scope, timeline, or delivery concerns must be sent to team leader copying Sales and CSM.

9.4 Project Tracking & Documentation
Objective: Maintain accurate engagement records at all times.
Steps:
Keep project tracker organized and up to date.
Document and update as information is known/changes:
Assigned SC
Deliverables Due - date
Deliverables Accepted - date
Forecasted go-live date
Key decisions (i.e. - ON HOLD, CANCELLED, etc )
Action items
Client dependencies
Ensure meetings are recorded and meeting notes/recaps are sent to the clients via email within 24 hours.

9.5 Risk & Issue Escalation
Objective: Identify risks early to protect delivery timelines.
Common Risk Indicators:
Delayed client data or prerequisites
Repeated rescheduling
Low client responsiveness
Confusion around scope or deliverables
Delayed Consulting assignment
Delayed handoff and implementation
Process:
Document the issue clearly.
Notify team lead with impact and context.
Track resolution status until closed (EC Action Item Tracker).

9.6 Billing & Administrative Support
Objective: Support accurate engagement execution and billing readiness.
Support Activities:
Monitor backlog report – review statuses, forecast dates, etc. ensuring downstream communication to those needed when ..
ICD missing when product activation communicated, incorrect due to Cancellation, On Hold, Shelfware, etc
Connect with Sales for any missing orders
Ensure DLSD is clearly communicated with the client especially if there are delays in implementation

9.7 End of Engagement
Objective: Ensure clean and complete engagement closure.
Steps:
Notify client of upcoming end of engagement, 2 weeks prior to estimated EoE date, including Sales Rep and CSM
Confirm all pending orders are completed or accounted for.
Send End of Engagement email. Include any pending products with assigned SC or POC. (End of Engagement Resources)
Log all challenges, issues encountered and accomplishments in our lessons learned file (EC Lessons Learned).

10. Out of Office Policy
Objective: Ensure coverage while EC is out of office

10.1 Planned Leaves Checklist

Get approval from Team Lead
Once approved log request in the PH PTO Request Portal
Secure out of office buddy/ies
Send OOO calendar invite to Leader and all ECs
Notify client at least a week in advance about upcoming leave
1-2 days before PTO
Notify SCs especially those with activations scheduled. Endorse expected activations or deliverables accordingly
Remind client about the upcoming PTO via email. Include pending items and OOO buddy details.
Setup a meeting with your buddy/ies to go over endorsements. Send a recap email detailing endorsements. Make sure TL is copied.
Setup your external and Internal OOO response. Template below.

Hello,
Thank you for reaching out. I am currently out of the office and will not be able to respond to emails until {date of return}. If you need assistance in the meantime, please contact {name, job title, cell phone number and email for alternative point of contact}. They will be able to assist you while I am away.
I appreciate your understanding and will respond to your message as soon I can upon my return.

Best regards,
{Your name}

10.2 Unplanned Leave Checklist

Notify your team leader
If with access and capacity
Setup OOO notification if possible (use standard OOO template)
Put an OOO blocker on your calendar
Notify your clients and SCs
Reach out to colleagues who can be your out of office buddy/ies

Note: Make calendar visible to your EC colleagues and editable to your team lead.

11. Continuous Improvement
Engagement Coordinators are expected to:
Identify process inefficiencies
Recommend workflow or tracking improvements
Participate in team training and knowledge sharing (Lessons Learned)
