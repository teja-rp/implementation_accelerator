This is an example of the conversion and go live timeline. The prior slide was intended to give a macro view on how all products are onboarding. This slide is a zoomed in view on the actual go live month. The site(s) will go into conversion on a Monday and they will be delivered back to you the following Wednesday. During the conversion period, the site staff will need to track activity manually. They will not have access to a property management system or any other functions that previously lived within your prop mgmt system, such as Screening. No data such as payments, move-ins/outs, or background checks should be done in your old system during the dark period. . Your site staff will begin catch up work once the conversion is complete and the site is delivered back you. And we will "go live" will all products in Phase 1 from prior slide, the following day.
**If the property is a COMBO property (ex. HUD/Tax combo), they will have an extra week of conversions, so that property wouldn't come out of conversion until the following Wednesday (22nd in the example above)
